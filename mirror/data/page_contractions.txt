# page: contractions
# rule: contraction-expansion
# The lesson: a contraction expands to its full form. Whole-word rows
# for the irregulars (won't is will+not, not wo+not); suffix rows for
# the regular clitic endings. 'd is honestly ambiguous (would or had)
# and says so. These tiny classes (n't 20 types, 'll 20, 'd 17,
# 've 14, 're 10, 'm 2 in the lexicon) are PAGE-TAUGHT, never mined.
